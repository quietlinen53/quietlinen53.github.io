Privacy Policy

Music Share. Effective 12 November 2026.

Music Share is a local test used for learning. It does not collect personal data from anyone, and it does not need data from other people or from other devices in order to function.

There is no account, analytics, advertising, remote database, or report sent to the person who wrote the application. That person receives nothing from the application. There is no monetary or other gain, and no data is gathered for that purpose.

The only thing stored is the Discord bot token, and only if the person using the application types it into the page. It is written to a private file at ~/.config/music-share/token on the computer where the application is run. It is not sent to the author and it is not stored anywhere else by this application.

If a voice channel is joined and playback is started, the audio that was chosen is sent to Discord so the channel can hear it. This application does not keep a copy of that audio and does not send it to anyone except Discord, as part of the action that was requested. Other people in the channel are not asked for information, and none of their information is collected.

Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or kept for a profile. To remove what this application stored, delete ~/.config/music-share/token, leave the voice channel, and remove the bot from the server. There is no other copy to delete and no request to send, because the author has no data.
